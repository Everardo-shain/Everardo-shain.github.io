Publications

Multi-Factor and Odometric Aware Optimization for Next-Best-View 3D Reconstruction
Under Review
IEEE Access Submitted 5 August 2026
Everardo Shain-Ruvalcaba Efraín López-Damian

This journal article (Q1 at the time of submission) extends prior work on the Next Best View (NBV) problem. It has two main contributions, a retuned multi-factor objective function (combining area, motion, and quality factors) and a robust optimization approach that explicitly accounts for odometric error. Robustness is achieved via a weighted combination of the nominal and expected objective function values, allowing the optimizer to counteract the effects of possible odometer errors. The approach is validated across two optimization methods (Simulated Annealing and an Evolution Strategy) and multiple simulated environments of varying complexity, showing that the benefit of robust optimization depends on scene complexity.

journal article next best view objective function 3D reconstruction robust optimization
Real-Time Detection of Cloned Spanish Audio in Telephone Calls Using Microcontrollers and Cloud Services
Under Review
ACM Transactions on Privacy and Security Submitted 30 April 2026
Ruben Villicaña Diana Paola Ayala-Roldan Everardo Shain-Ruvalcaba Alfredo Mantilla-Caeiros Alejandro Santos-Diaz Luis Montesinos

This journal article (Q1 at the time of submission) presents the development and evaluation of a real-time system for detecting cloned Spanish audio during telephone calls. A comprehensive dataset of real and cloned recordings was created, encoded in four standard telephone formats (GSM, $\mu$-law, a-law, and G.723.1) and enhanced with varying noise levels. The proposed architecture leverages microcontrollers and Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud services for real-time analysis using deep learning models. The system’s performance was validated against human capability, demonstrating high accuracy in real-world scenarios and outperforming participants’ ability to distinguish between real and cloned audio samples.

journal article deep learning audio deepfakes embedded systems cloud computing (AWS) signal processing cybersecurity
Tuning and Comparison of Optimization Algorithms for the Next Best View Problematic
Published
IEEE Access (Volume: 12) 9 December 2024
Everardo Shain-Ruvalcaba Efraín López-Damian

This journal article (Q1 at the time of publication) presents the tuning and comparison of various optimization algorithms on the Next Best View (NBV) problem. A simulated 5 Degree-of-Freedom (DOF) mobile robot with a mounted simulated range sensor was used on a Virtual Reality Modeling Language (VRML) environment, and the space discretization was made using a voxel map. The optimization methods tested were Nelder-Mead, an Evolution Strategy, and Simulated Annealing. Their repeatability was tested on a laboratory model, a room with a cube and a pyramid inside it, and a study room with multiple furniture and windows.

journal article next best view objective function optimization algorithms 3D reconstruction

View planning for three-dimensional environment reconstruction using the Next Best View method
Published
Instituto Tecnológico y de Estudios Superiores de Monterrey 3 December 2024
Everardo Shain-Ruvalcaba Efraín López-Damian

This master’s thesis describes the impact of the objective function and optimization methods on the Next Best View problem, which consists in finding the next position that the sensor or camera needs to take to scan an object or scenery in its totality. A multi-factor objective function was designed including area and motion factors. Global optimization tasks such as a backstepping technique to escape local minima and a dynamic change in the objective function were implemented. Optimization methods such as Nelder-Mead, an Evolution Strategy, and Simulated Annealing were tuned and compared on three different scenarios.
